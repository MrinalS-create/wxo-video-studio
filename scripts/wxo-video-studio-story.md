## Narration

Two open-source tools. One pipeline. Zero video editing skills required.

Kokoro is an open-weight text-to-speech model with 82 million parameters. It runs fully offline on your machine, requires no API key, and is free to use under the Apache 2.0 license. Give it a sentence. It gives you a voice.

HyperFrames is a headless video renderer. Write your scenes in HTML and CSS, animate them with GSAP, and HyperFrames captures every frame and exports a production-quality MP4. Also Apache 2.0 — free for any use.

Neither tool is usable by a designer without significant engineering setup. So I used IBM Bob to build the glue layer. A Kokoro TTS wrapper. An animated scene template with brand tokens. A one-command setup script. And a Bob skill that orchestrates the whole pipeline. Describe your feature in plain text. Bob generates the voice, builds the scenes, syncs the audio, and renders the video.

Write the script. Bob handles the rest.
