# Prompt: Edit an existing video

Use this after you've watched the rendered video and want to make changes.
Copy, paste into Bob, and fill in the [brackets].

---

```
I want to edit the video at output/[feature-slug].mp4

Here are my changes:
[describe what you want changed — see examples below]

Please re-render to output/[feature-slug].mp4 when done.
```

---
